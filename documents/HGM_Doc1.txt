3. Hand Glove Making - Document 1: Selecting Leather and Cutting Patterns

The foundation of high-quality gloves lies in selecting the right leather. Common choices include lambskin for luxury gloves, cowhide for durability, and pigskin for breathable utility gloves. The leather must be tanned and softened before cutting.

Patterns are drawn based on hand measurements, ensuring precise finger lengths and palm width. Traditional glove-making uses a “trank” pattern—a single piece forming the palm and fingers—along with fourchettes (narrow strips between fingers) and quirks (small triangular inserts at finger bases for flexibility).

Cutting is done using sharp knives or hydraulic cutters to maintain clean edges. The grain direction of leather should always follow the length of the fingers for proper elasticity.
