9. Woodworking - Document 1: Joint Types and Cutting Techniques

Woodworking relies heavily on precise joinery to create strong, stable structures. 
Common joints include the mortise and tenon, dovetail, and lap joint. 
Each serves a different structural purpose: dovetails for drawers, mortise and tenon for frames, and lap joints for simple connections.

Cutting is done using hand saws, chisels, or power tools such as table saws and routers. 
Proper marking with a square and marking gauge ensures accuracy. 
Grain orientation should always be considered to prevent splitting during cutting and assembly.

Sharpening tools regularly is essential for clean, accurate cuts.
