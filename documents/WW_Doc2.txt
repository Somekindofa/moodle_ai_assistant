10. Woodworking - Document 2: Sanding, Finishing, and Protection

After assembly, sanding is performed to smooth surfaces and remove tool marks. 
Starting with coarse grit (e.g., 80) and progressing to fine grit (e.g., 220) provides a professional finish.

Wood finishes such as oil, varnish, or lacquer are then applied for protection and aesthetics. 
Oils penetrate the wood fibers, while varnishes form a hard protective layer. 
Between coats, light sanding ensures adhesion and smoothness.

Finally, waxing or polishing can add an extra layer of luster and protection, ensuring durability against moisture and wear.
