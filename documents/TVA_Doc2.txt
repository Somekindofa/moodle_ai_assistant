8. TV Assembly - Document 2: Testing and Quality Assurance

Once assembled, the TV goes through multiple testing phases. First is the power-on test, ensuring that the backlight and mainboard communicate properly. Then, signal integrity tests verify HDMI ports, USB connections, and Wi-Fi functionality.

Color calibration is performed to adjust brightness, contrast, and color balance to meet factory standards. Automated optical inspection systems may be used to detect pixel defects or screen uniformity issues.

Finally, the unit undergoes a burn-in test where it operates continuously for several hours to confirm reliability before packaging.
